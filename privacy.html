PRIVACY POLICY & DATA DIRECTIVE

1. DATA COLLECTION & PERSONAL IDENTITY

OBSCURA RECORDS respects your personal privacy. We collect only essential connection information, artist identification, and transmission payloads required to deliver label services, evaluate incoming demo submissions, manage direct communications, and administer audio releases. We never sell, rent, or distribute your personal data or identity to third-party marketing networks.

2. DEMO SUBMISSIONS & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All official content across the OBSCURA domain—including audio recordings, stems, graphics, branding logos, visual artwork, and artist profiles—is protected by international copyright laws. When you submit unreleased music through the submission portal, you retain 100% of your underlying intellectual property rights until a formal, mutually executed release agreement is signed. We treat all incoming demo links and audio stems with strict confidentiality.

3. DSP PLATFORMS & THIRD-PARTY EMBEDS

Our portals interface with global streaming distribution networks (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, TikTok, SoundCloud, and Beatport). When you click external smartlinks or listen through integrated streaming audio players, your engagement is subject to the respective privacy policies, terms of service, and cookie protocols of those independent platforms.

4. PROMOTIONAL TRANSMISSIONS & CAMPAIGNS

Artists and community members who connect with our portal or opt in to communications may receive notifications regarding upcoming drops, playlist placements, merchandise launches, and viral network promotion campaigns. You may request removal from our contact directory or unsubscribe at any time.

5. DATA RETENTION & ENCRYPTION PROTOCOLS

All messages, submission files, and transmission metadata are stored on encrypted cloud infrastructure guarded by TLS security protocols and Google reCAPTCHA authentication. We retain transmission data only for the period necessary for artist evaluation, legal compliance, and community record-keeping.

6. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION

All operational directives, intellectual property rights management, artist contracts, and legal inquiries are governed under international copyright conventions and digital media regulations.

7. LEGAL CONTACT, DMCA & DATA INQUIRIES

For all legal inquiries, copyright complaints (DMCA), data deletion requests, licensing terms, or general rights management, contact the OBSCURA legal administration directly at:

artists@obscurarecord.com
